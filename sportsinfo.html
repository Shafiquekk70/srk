Football

football, game in which two teams of 11 players, using any part of their bodies except their hands and arms, try to maneuver the ball into the opposing team’s goal.

Cricket

Cricket is played with bat and ball. Two teams compete to score runs. Bowlers aim to dismiss the batsmen. Fielders guard the boundary with speed. Fans cheer as the game brings joy.

Tennis

Tennis is played between two or four players. The aim is to hit the ball over the net into the opponent’s court. Points are scored when the opponent cannot return the ball.

Swimming

Swimming is a sport and a life skill. It is done in pools, rivers, lakes, or the sea. Swimmers use strokes like freestyle, backstroke, breaststroke, and butterfly. It builds strength, stamina, and keeps the body fit.

Chess

Chess is a strategic board game played between two players. The board has 64 squares arranged in black and white. Each player commands 16 pieces with unique moves. The aim is to checkmate the opponent’s king.

Basketball

Basketball is a fast‑paced team sport played worldwide. Two teams aim to score by shooting the ball into a hoop. Each team has five players on the court at a time. Dribbling, passing, and shooting are the key skills.

Hockey

Hockey is a fast and energetic team sport. Players use sticks to hit a ball or puck. The aim is to score goals against the opponent. It requires speed, skill, and teamwork. Hockey is played on fields, ice rinks, and even indoors.
