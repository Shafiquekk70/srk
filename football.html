About Football

Football, also known as soccer, is the world's most popular sport, played between two teams of 11 players each.

Each match is 90 minutes long, divided into two halves of 45 minutes each.

The objective is to score more goals than the opponent by getting the ball into the opposing team's net.

Football is played professionally worldwide and is governed by FIFA rules.

Lionel Messi

One of the greatest footballers of all time, Messi is known for his dribbling, vision, and goal-scoring ability.

He has won multiple Ballon d'Or awards and numerous league titles with FC Barcelona and PSG.
